Full site audit and P0/P1 sweep

Audited production (www.ultimateffcs.in, PR #19 live) on 18 Oct 2026, phone first at 375×812, then 1440 wide, light and dark. Fixes are on branch fix/audit-sweep (15 commits on origin/main), tested locally and not pushed or deployed.

22P2 reported
7P3 reported

The worst problem was in the generator itself. With the audit's own course set (BCSE204L/P, BCSE209L/P, BCSE206L), the search stopped at 500 professor permutations of the first week it found. It showed 12 of the 54 possible weeks, called a 49.1 week "Best overall" when a 52.8 week existed, and only BCSE204L/P could be swapped. That is fixed, with a test that fails on the old code.

Two more P0s could lose a student's work. On the homepage, tapping your own campus card wiped the whole plan. And the saved state was large enough to hit Safari's storage limit with a bigger course list, after which every action quietly stopped saving.

Couldn't test, and why

  • Real phone and iOS Safari: no device here. The storage-quota fix was checked by forcing a quota in Chrome: no errors, results shown, saves persisted.
  • Slow 4G run and Cancel mid-run: the browser pane can't throttle the network, and generation finishes in about 2s, too fast to cancel by hand. Lighthouse's simulated slow 4G stands in for the slow run.
  • Google Calendar import and WhatsApp preview: both would act on your accounts. I checked the .ics contents (every event on its own weekday) and the OG tags and image instead.
  • Stored usage-event rows: a read-only query on the production database was blocked by the permission system. The code only stores an allowlisted name, campus and props (no IP or id columns). Blocking /api/events wasn't tested live; track() is fire-and-forget inside try/catch.
  • Admin dashboard signed in: entering the admin password is off-limits for me. Only the logged-out protection was checked.
  • Catalog vs VTOP spot-check: no VTOP access.
  • Every page in both themes at both widths: partial. All pages at 375 dark; Home and Planner at 1440 light and dark; light-mode contrast measured on the planner.
  • Returning-visitor run with a saved plan was done; the slow-connection run (third pass) was not.

Top 3 UX fixes after mock FFCS

  1. Put the week switcher first on phones. Today a student sees one week, then up to 94 professor combos, and only then the other weeks. Put the weeks in a horizontal strip under the header and fold the combos into a "Professors for this week" section.
  2. Let Swap build any combination. Build the swapped week on demand instead of picking from precomputed variants, so any pair of professors is reachable. Then rename "shapes / combos / professor options" to two words students already use: weeks and professors.
  3. Show what a rule costs before Find my weeks. A live line under each preference ("No 8 AM removes all 9 BCSE209L options in B1") and a real free-day picker would catch the zero-results surprise in the planner, where it's cheap to fix.
